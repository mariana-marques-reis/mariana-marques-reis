# Hi, I'm Mariana

**R&D Engineer** at Instituto Pedro Nunes — LAS (Laboratory for Automatics and Systems), Coimbra, Portugal.

I build systems where **AI meets 3D and simulation** — generative 3D pipelines, local LLM infrastructure, and physics-based digital twins.

MSc in Electrical and Computer Engineering (Robotics, Control and Artificial Intelligence) and BSc in Engineering Physics, University of Coimbra.

- **AI Infrastructure** — GPU compute, Docker, Kubernetes (k3s), GitOps
